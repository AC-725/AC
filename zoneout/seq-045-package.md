## READ THIS BEFORE YOU POST

**Never tell anyone to wash a garment labelled `Dryclean only`.** That was the original
angle for this video and it is FALSE — the FTC rule requires a reasonable basis for BOTH
the drycleaning instruction AND the warning against washing. The correction runs soft to
strong, never strong to soft. If a commenter reads it the other way, correct them.

## INSTAGRAM CAPTION — paste as-is

Dry clean and dry clean only are two different labels.

A plain "Dryclean" instruction only requires the manufacturer to name ONE safe cleaning
method. The FTC's own guidance says drycleaning "need not be the only, or even the best,
method of cleaning the item." It is simply the method they chose to print.

"Dryclean only" is the stronger claim. It needs a reasonable basis for the drycleaning
instruction AND for the warning against washing.

So "only" is not padding. It is the part somebody had to substantiate.

Go read the label inside your collar.
