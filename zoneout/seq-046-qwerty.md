## The adversarial pass changed this video

The plan was "the keyboard was designed to slow you down — here is who wrote that down."
**There is no such person to name.** The pass found:

- The **jam-prevention** account is mainstream and partly true — keys likely to be struck
  in sequence were split across the board so type bars would not clash.
- **Yasuoka & Yasuoka (Kyoto, 2011), "On the Prehistory of QWERTY"** dispute even that,
  arguing the layout came from telegraph operators transcribing Morse.
- **Nobody names who first wrote "designed to slow typists."** Absence of a finding is not
  evidence of absence, and the script must not pretend otherwise.

So sources disagree on the cause. What every account agrees on is narrower and still
surprising: **slowing the typist was never the goal.** That is the claim the video makes.

The strongest beat is mechanism, not authority. Yasuoka, verbatim: *"The speed of Morse
receiver should be equal to the Morse sender, of course. If Sholes really arranged the
keyboard to slow down the operator, the operator became unable to catch up the Morse
sender."* A deliberately slow operator breaks the machine. No citation needed to feel it.

## `trap` — what NOT to say

**1. Never say the telegraph origin is settled.** Beat 3 says *they traced*, which is
provenance, not verdict — the same construction seq 44 used for the 2015 Brontosaurus
paper. The mainstream account is still type-bar jam prevention. Say either is proven and
the video is false.

**2. Never name who started the slow-down story.** No source does. "Nobody could find who
started it" is also an overreach — it claims a negative nobody established.

**3. Do not say QWERTY is slow, or that Dvorak is faster.** Liebowitz & Margolis found the
evidence for Dvorak's superiority extremely thin, and the most favourable studies were
commissioned or run by Dvorak himself — who was selling the rival layout. That is a
separate video and a good one; it is not this one.

**4. The jam story is not simply wrong.** Splitting common letter pairs across the board is
documented. What is wrong is "the goal was to slow you down."
