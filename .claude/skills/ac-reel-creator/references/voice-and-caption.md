# Voice, story-pick, caption, hashtags, VO — both formats

The visuals are systematized, so the words are the variable that matters. Everything is
written in AC's voice: **AI in plain English, no hype, no jargon.** If a 12-year-old
couldn't follow scene A, rewrite it. Full brand voice: `ac-instagram` Part 2.

## AC's voice
- Direct and plain. Short lines. One idea per scene.
- Contrarian but constructive: challenge the obvious take, then hand over a better one.
  Never doom — always a way forward.
- Anchored to one real number per beat, matching the source's own wording.
- Anti-hype: no "this changes everything", no "the most powerful", no fear kickers.
- Encouraging, a little wry, never condescending. The recurring lesson: AI isn't magic,
  it's leverage — and leverage only pays the people who pull it. Start with one boring task.
- No em-dashes on screen, ever. Periods, commas, or line breaks.

## Picking the story / tool (verify before you write)
**AI News:** a current, real headline with a concrete number a normal business owner can
act on. Verify the key figure from 1–2 credible sources; match wording. Screen out pure
hype, rumors, and anything with no plain-English "so what."
**Tool of the Day:** current news hook (launch, big update, went-free), positive for
general + business users, teachable in ~3 steps, concrete payoff (they end with a thing).
**Both:** favor broadly relevant angles that are inherently sendable — "send this to
someone who…" is the top 2026 ranking signal (see `algorithm-2026.md`).

## Caption formula (five beats + the SEO line)
**Line 1 is a search phrase** a target viewer would type ("AI tools for small business",
"AI news for business owners") folded naturally into the hook line — captions now act as
search queries (social SEO). Then:
1. **Hook** — punchy, usually the scene-A thesis, anchored to the number.
2. **Reframe** — flip the obvious reading.
3. **Plain-English breakdown** — 2–4 short beats; News: old-way/new-way with sources in a
   parenthetical; TOD: the 3-step how-to with → arrows.
4. **The rule** — the memorable takeaway (echoes the video's rule scene).
5. **Soft CTA** — lead with a dash: Save this · Tag someone · Comment "WORD" · Follow the
   handle for daily AI in plain English. Never a hard sell.

Formatting: one big number, line breaks every 1–2 sentences, a question near the end to
pull comments, end the CTA with the handle from the template's `HANDLE` constant.

**Bilingual option (ask each run):** if AC wants HK reach, also ship the caption in
繁體中文 — natural, conversational HK tone, not a literal translation; keep the numbers,
the question, and the CTA. Hashtags stay in English.

## Hashtags — exactly 5, trending but rankable
Hashtags are a supporting signal now (keywords in the caption matter more), but still
ship 5, one per lane, sized so the post can rank (not only mega-tags):
- one **broad-topical**, currently trending in the AI lane (`#AInews`, `#ArtificialIntelligence`, `#AI2026`)
- one **capability**, tied to the story (`#AIautomation`, `#AIagents`, `#AItools`, `#NoCode`)
- one **category** (`#TechNews`, `#FutureOfWork`) — TOD may ride the tool's own tag (`#Canva`)
- two **audience** (`#SmallBusiness`, `#Entrepreneurs`, `#SmallBusinessOwner`, `#AIforBusiness`)
When picking the trending broad tag, do a quick search for what AI creators are using this
month rather than assuming.

## Voice-over script (standard deliverable, both formats)
Write a timed VO table (scene start → line), ~2.5–3 words/sec, in AC's voice:
- **News (~11.7s, 5 rows):** one short line per scene; punch one word in the open and one
  in the rule; the close is a nudge, not a pitch. ~30–35 words total.
- **TOD (~31.6s, 7 rows):** conversational walkthrough; scene E carries the 3 steps
  ("One… Two… Three…"); keep ~80–95 words total.
Include: a one-breath clean-read version, 2–3 delivery notes, and the instruction to
record over the `--silent` build so the voice doesn't fight the baked SFX (offer a ducked
mix as an alternative).

## A/B hook variants (standard deliverable)
Alongside the shipped scene-A, write 2 alternate hook lines (different hook styles from
`hooks.md`), as plain text with a one-line rationale each — for Instagram trial reels.

## Deliverables each run
MP4 (+ `--silent` cut when VO planned), cover PNG, `caption.txt` (SEO line + caption +
5 hashtags, + 繁體中文 if chosen), `vo_script.md`, `script.md` (per scene: on-screen /
visual / VO / SFX), `ab_hooks.txt`, posting note, hook grade + virality scorecard, and a
`run-log.md` entry.
