### Baseline metrics that decay — capture order matters `[obs]` 2026-07-31

1. **Search Console export, all four tabs** (clicks/impressions/position, Queries, Pages,
   Devices). 16-month rolling delete. **The only genuinely unrecoverable item.**
2. **GA4 export** (Acquisition, Pages and screens, Tech details) — and check the retention
   setting, commonly 2 or 14 months, so some may already be gone.
3. **A complete copy of the old site** — hosting backup (30-day rotation, call today), the
   previous vendor, or Wayback. Side-by-side screenshots outsell every number for this
   audience.
4. **Enquiry volume by month and source, 12 months** — shared inbox, phone log, and 30
   minutes with whoever answers the phone.
5. **The 20-enquiry question:** "of the last 20 enquiries, how many found you online versus
   were introduced?" `[claude]` If it's 18-of-20 referrals, this cannot honestly be a
   lead-gen case study — and AC needs to know that before writing a word.

Also: stopwatch the current enquiry process once, on a real enquiry. Not an estimate.
