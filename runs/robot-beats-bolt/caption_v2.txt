AI news for business owners: a humanoid robot just ran 100m faster than Usain Bolt.

On Saturday in Beijing, a robot called Tiangong Ultra ran the 100m in 9.39 seconds.

Usain Bolt's record is 9.58. It has stood since 2009.

Then it hit the crash pad at the end of the lane, went down, and was carried off on a stretcher. Bolt jogged a victory lap in Berlin.

Two things nobody is putting on a slide.

First: no independent body ratified that time. Bolt's was ratified by World Athletics. This one was timed by the same industry that built the robot. Fast, yes. Official, no.

Second: it was a preliminary heat, not a final. The robot was superhuman at the one thing it was measured on, and had no idea what to do the second the measured part ended.

That is most AI in a business right now. Brilliant inside the task. Blind at the edges of it.

So when you hand something to AI, don't just ask what it can do. Ask what happens the second it finishes.

Fast is not the same as finished.
