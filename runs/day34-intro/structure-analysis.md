## What the video itself does (from AC's screen recording, 21.7s)

- **0–1s:** seated talking head, red caption **"This is a message to all
  universities"** — the warning opens by naming its TARGET, not the viewer.
- **~2–8s:** movie-trailer montage: full-screen red serif caps, **one word per cut**
  ("IS… MILLIONAIRE… AND… DOWN… YOUR…"), alternating close/wide shots and
  dark-moody vs white-backdrop lighting. Cut density is extreme; the lighting flip is
  the "architecture change".
- **~9–15s:** wordless b-roll flex beats — fanned cash, outfit, crossed-arm stances.
  Pure cinematography, zero information.
- **~16–20s:** **tone flip**: his editor leans into frame, yellow subtitles — "My boss
  thinks he's famous… we made a documentary." Self-deprecation is the release valve
  that makes the preceding arrogance likable.
- **~21s:** wide standing shot, white backdrop. The reel contains almost no substance;
  everything is gated behind the caption's comment keyword.

## The skeleton (what actually does the work)

| # | Move | How Squibb executes it | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Callout warning hook** | "you have been WARNED… 😡" — high-arousal, direct address, stakes implied before topic | Indignation is a top-tier share emotion; the warning frame makes the viewer the protagonist |
| 2 | **Contrarian claim on a mass-identity topic** | University = scam. Attacks a default life path his young audience already privately doubts | The audience feels *seen*, not lectured; the claim is disputable, so it stays open |
| 3 | **The reel is a trailer, not the payload** | The reel sells the "full doc" — a bigger asset that lives elsewhere | The reel doesn't have to resolve; incompleteness is the retention device |
| 4 | **Comment-keyword gate** | `Comment "UNI" to watch the full doc` | Trades the payoff for a comment. Every "UNI" is an algorithm signal AND a DM-automation contact. This single line produced ~9K comments |
| 5 | **Identity question** | `Has uni scammed you? 👇` | Second, independent comment driver — fills the thread with *stories*, not just keywords, which keeps the comment section alive and human |
| 6 | (Squibb team practice) | 3 hooks in the first seconds; caption never baked into the edit | Documented in his team's own growth writeup — every video gets multiple chances to stop the thumb |

Two comment mechanisms **stacked** (keyword + question) is the signature. Most creators
run one. The keyword feeds the funnel; the question feeds the culture.

## Translation to @itsac.ai (the calibration, not a copy)

Squibb's fuel is rage at an institution. AC's brand law bans doom and fear-farming — but
the *skeleton* transfers cleanly, because AC has his own villain that deserves a warning:
**AI hype content itself.** The page's founding promise ("no hype, no jargon, every
number checked") maps move-for-move:

| Squibb move | @itsac.ai intro version |
|---|---|
| "This is a message to all universities" | Kicker **"To the AI gurus"** — the warning names its villain first; the viewer eavesdrops (adds the Gossip send motive) |
| One-word-per-cut trailer montage | The engine equivalent: typed subtitles + the ADVICE→ADS strike-toggle. Cut density stays the engine's law (an onset every 2–4s), not 15 hard cuts |
| Editor's deadpan outro ("My boss thinks he's famous") | The caption's tone-flip line: "(Yes, the warning tone is dramatic. The receipts aren't.)" — same release valve, keeps the anti-hype page from sounding self-serious |
| Uni is a scam | **Most AI advice is an ad.** Disputable, mass-identity (everyone's feed is full of it), and it's literally the page's founding claim |
| The full doc | **The page itself is the doc** — plus a gated starter kit as the immediate asset |
| Comment "UNI" | **Comment "PLAIN"** → DM the plain-English starter kit (3 checks + first prompt + the one rule) |
| "Has uni scammed you?" | **"What's the last AI promise that turned out to be an ad?"** — self-identification, story-bait |
| Aggression via volume | Aggression via **specificity** (hooks.md v2: credible hooks out-aggress hype hooks on exactly one dimension) — receipts on screen: 14 "free" tools, 6 were trials; the 80% follow-up stat nobody can source |

Where Squibb teases ("watch the full doc"), AC **proves first, then gates** — the reel
shows two receipts before asking for anything. That's the anti-hype inversion: the
warning is only credible on this page because the check is shown, not claimed.

Both receipt numbers are self-sourced from logged, verified runs (Day 28 free-tier
check; Day 25 killed-stat hunt) — recycling them here is deliberate: an introduction's
proof *should* be the page's own track record.
