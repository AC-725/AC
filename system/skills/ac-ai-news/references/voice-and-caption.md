# Voice, caption & hashtags — AI News reel

The visuals are systematized, so the words are the variable that matters. Write everything in
AC's voice: **AI in plain English, no hype, no jargon.** If a 12-year-old couldn't follow scene
A, rewrite it. For the full brand voice defer to the `ac-instagram` skill (Part 2); this is the
short version tuned for a news reel.

## On-screen voice (the video copy)
- Direct and plain. Short lines. One idea per scene.
- Contrarian but constructive: challenge the obvious take, then hand over a better one. Never doom.
- Anchored to one real number per scene, matching the source's own wording.
- Anti-hype: no "this changes everything", no "the most powerful", no fear kickers.
- No em-dashes on screen, ever. Periods, commas, or line breaks.

## Pick the story (verify before you write)
Good AI-news reel material: a current, real headline with a concrete number, that a normal
business owner can act on or should understand. Web-search it fresh (never rely on memory for
"what happened"), and verify the key figure from 1–2 credible sources before it goes on screen.
Screen out pure hype, unverifiable rumors, and anything with no plain-English "so what".

## Caption formula (mirrors AC's proven post arc)
Write the caption in five beats, longer than the on-screen copy is allowed to be:
1. **Hook** — one punchy line, often the same thesis as scene A, anchored to the number.
2. **Reframe** — flip the obvious reading of the news.
3. **Plain-English breakdown** — 2–4 short beats, one concrete example or an old-way/new-way.
4. **The rule** — the memorable takeaway (can echo scene D).
5. **Soft CTA** — lead with a dash, then one of: Save this · Tag someone · Comment "X" ·
   Follow @itsac.ai for daily AI in plain English. Never a hard sell in feed.
Formatting: one big number, line breaks every 1–2 sentences for mobile, a question near the end
to pull comments.

## Hashtags (exactly 5)
Pick 5, one from each lane, sized so the post can actually rank (avoid only mega-tags):
- one **broad-topical** (#AInews, #ArtificialIntelligence)
- one **capability** (tie to the story: #AIautomation, #AItools)
- one **category** (#TechNews, #FutureOfWork)
- two **audience** (#SmallBusiness, #Entrepreneurs, #SmallBusinessOwner)

## Deliverables each run
MP4 (front-loaded, synced sound), a `caption.txt` (caption + 5 hashtags), and a short
shot-by-shot `script.md` (per scene: on-screen copy / visual / SFX). Cover graphic is optional
for a news reel; offer it, don't force it.
