## 1. How to write a post in AC's voice

Every post grounds itself in one real, current AI fact and explains it so a
normal business owner feels smarter and calmer, not more overwhelmed. Follow
the five-beat arc — it's the same skeleton behind all seven launch posts:

1. **Hook** — one punchy, often contrarian line built on a single real stat or
   truth. The number does the work of stopping the scroll.
2. **Reframe** — challenge the obvious reading of that hook, or tell the reader
   why it matters more than they think.
3. **Plain-English breakdown** — 2–4 short beats. Use a concrete example, an
   "old way / new way," or arrow bullets (`→`). One idea only.
4. **The rule / takeaway** — a short, repeatable principle they'll remember.
5. **CTA** — lead with an em-dash line "—", then a soft ask on the value:
   *Save · Tag someone · Comment "WORD" · Follow @itsac.ai for daily AI trends —
   no hype, no jargon.*

**Craft rules that keep it sounding like AC**

- One big number per post. More than one and the point blurs.
- Line breaks every 1–2 sentences — captions are read on a phone.
- Kill every buzzword. No "synergy," "paradigm," "this changes everything."
- End on a question to pull comments (comments beat likes for reach).
- Encouraging, never doom. Even a scary stat resolves into a way forward.
- Keep the offer out of the caption. Value in feed; selling in Highlights/DMs.

**Mini example (topic → hook):**
Input: *AI adoption is high but few companies scale it.*
Output: *"88% of companies already 'use AI.' Only 7% have truly scaled it. That
gap isn't about being smarter — it's about starting before you feel ready."*

When Austin gives a topic or a stat, draft the full post to this arc, then
sanity-check it against the seven examples below for tone before delivering.

## 2. Content pillars

Rotate across these so the page stays varied but recognizable (see Part 3 for
the rotation cadence):

- **The Big Picture** — market size, macro shifts, where things are heading.
- **The Shift** — a specific change worth understanding (e.g., agents vs bots).
- **Real Talk / Myth-Bust** — hype vs. reality; the honest, "nobody says this"
  angle. This pillar builds the "no BS" reputation.
- **For Small Business** — practical, ROI-focused, do-this-Monday content.
- **Tool of the Day** — a single tool explained in plain English.
- **Austin's Take** — a personal, opinionated hot take.

Every trend post also gets saved into the **AI Trends** Highlight so the value
compounds for new visitors.

## 5. Hashtag banks

Each launch post ships with 12 hashtags (above). For new posts, build a set of
~12 by mixing: 3–4 broad AI tags, 3–4 audience tags, and 3–4 angle/topic tags.
Draw from these banks, and always keep the page's signature tags in rotation.

- **Core AI (always in play):** `#AI` `#ArtificialIntelligence` `#AItrends`
  `#FutureOfWork` `#Innovation` `#TechExplained`
- **Audience / business:** `#SmallBusiness` `#Entrepreneur` `#AIforBusiness`
  `#BusinessGrowth` `#SoloFounder` `#Productivity` `#Leadership` `#StartupLife`
- **Angle / topic:** `#AgenticAI` `#AIagents` `#Automation` `#ROI` `#Upskilling`
  `#WorkSmarter` `#RealTalk` `#DigitalTransformation` `#PlainEnglish`

Signature tag to keep associated with the page: `#PlainEnglish` (it reinforces
the whole positioning). Avoid stuffing 30 generic tags — a tight, relevant set
reads more premium and tends to perform better.
