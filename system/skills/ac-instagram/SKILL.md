# AC — Instagram Business Protocol

This is the operating manual for Austin's Instagram brand. Its job is to make
sure every post, caption, graphic, and growth move stays consistent — because
on Instagram, consistency is what turns a grid into a brand. Read this page
first, then open whichever Part the task needs.

## The brand in one breath

- **Name / brand:** AC — *AI in Plain English*
- **Person:** Austin (goes by AC)
- **Handle:** `@itsac.ai` — the one handle, on every surface. No alternates.
- **Home base:** `itsac.ai` — Austin helps regular businesses (not tech giants)
  actually use AI. Hong Kong → worldwide. (Moved from `ac-wins.com` to match the
  handle. Confirm the site resolves before pointing the live bio link at it; until
  it does, a link hub is the safer target. See `<repo>/system/foundation/brand.md`.)
- **The promise:** *Making AI make sense for normal businesses.* One real AI
  market trend every day, in plain English, zero jargon.
- **The feeling:** premium, calm, confident. Black canvas, one gold idea,
  elegant serif headline. Think "quiet luxury," not "hustle bro."

## Positioning — what makes this page different

The internet is drowning in AI hype. AC is the antidote: the person who
translates AI from noise into things that actually move a business. Every piece
of content should reinforce one reputation — **"this person makes AI make
sense."** No buzzwords, no "this changes everything," no fear-mongering. Just
one clear, useful idea a day, grounded in real data.

AC's own framing: *"Making everything user friendly. And I am the connector for
this."* He is the connector between ordinary people and AI that already exists —
not a builder, not a researcher. Content should read as a connection being made,
never as a lecture.

## Who you're writing to

**Full definition:** `<repo>/system/foundation/audience.md` — read it before
writing for a new angle or audience segment.

The short version: they are **not technical and don't want to become technical.**
That's the one trait shared across general/beginner users, non-technical small
business owners, solo operators, ops managers inside SMEs, and aspiring AI
consultants. They want hours back on boring work, reassurance they're not being
left behind, and something they can use today.

**Not for:** engineers and AI builders (they want to build; this page says don't
build, just use), or hype-chasers (they want "this changes everything," which is
the opposite of the promise).

Default when a post can only serve one group: **write for the business owner.**
Larger ring, clearer buyer — and the aspiring consultant learns by watching.

## Voice — how AC sounds (read this before writing anything)

The voice is the most important asset here, because the visuals are already
systematized. When you write for this page, sound like this:

- **Direct and plain-spoken.** Short sentences. No jargon. If a 12-year-old
  couldn't follow it, rewrite it.
- **Contrarian but constructive.** Open by challenging the obvious take, then
  give people a better one. Never doom — always a way forward.
- **Grounded in one real number.** Each post is anchored to a genuine, current
  stat or fact. The number earns the reader's attention; the plain-English
  explanation keeps it.
- **Encouraging, not preachy.** Austin is the guide who's been there — "I'll be
  here daily to help you not quit." Warm, a little wry, never condescending.
- **Anti-hype, pro-action.** The recurring lesson: AI isn't magic, it's
  leverage — and leverage only pays the people who actually pull it. Start
  before you feel ready; start with one boring, painful, repetitive task.
- **Never salesy in-feed.** Value pulls people in. Working-together talk lives
  only in Highlights and DMs, never in daily posts.

## The post formula (AC's proven structure)

Every strong post on this page follows the same arc. Use it as the default
skeleton when generating new content:

1. **Hook** — a punchy, often contrarian line anchored to one real stat or
   truth. ("Nobody posts this part: 40% of AI agent projects will be scrapped
   by 2027." / "$1 in. $3.50 out.")
2. **Reframe** — flip the obvious reading. ("Sounds like bad news. It's
   actually the most useful stat I'll share all week.")
3. **Plain-English breakdown** — 2–4 short beats with a concrete example, an
   "old way / new way," or arrows. One idea, explained simply.
4. **The rule / takeaway** — a memorable, repeatable principle. ("Small wins
   compound. Hype evaporates." / "AI isn't magic. It's leverage.")
5. **CTA** — soft, on the value. Lead with an em-dash "—", then one of:
   *Save this · Tag someone · Comment "X" · Follow @itsac.ai for daily AI trends
   — no hype, no jargon.*

Formatting tics that make it feel like AC: one big number per post, line breaks
every 1–2 sentences for mobile readability, a question near the end to pull
comments, and ~30 words max if the line is going *on the graphic* (the caption
can be longer).

## Non-negotiables (the brand guardrails)

These are the few hard rules — everything else is judgment. They exist because
the repetition *is* the branding; break them and the page stops looking like a
brand.

- One gold accent per post. Gold means "look here." Never add other colors,
  stock photos, or clip-art.
- Never change the fonts (Lora for headlines, Poppins for labels/body).
- Plain English only. Kill every buzzword and every "this changes everything."
- No hard selling in feed. The CTA ladder is Save → Follow → Comment → DM; the
  offer only appears in Highlights and DMs.
- Show up daily. The algorithm — and the audience — reward reliability over
  intensity.
