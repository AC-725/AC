# Voice, content & caption formula

This skill produces one thing: a **Tool of the Day** Reel for @itsac.ai ("AI in Plain English").
It sits inside the wider `ac-instagram` brand protocol — if that skill is available, its
Part 2 (Content) is the source of truth for voice. This file is the working recap.

## The voice (how AC sounds)
- Plain-spoken, short sentences, zero jargon. If a 12-year-old couldn't follow it, rewrite.
- Contrarian but constructive: challenge the obvious take, then give a better one. Never doom.
- Anchored to one real number. Anti-hype, pro-action: "AI isn't magic, it's leverage."
- Encouraging, a little wry, never salesy in-feed. The offer lives in DMs/Highlights, never the post.

## What makes a good "Tool of the Day" pick
Research current AI news (always web-search — don't trust memory for what's "new") and choose a tool that is:
1. **Current** — a real, recent news hook (a launch, a big update, went-free). Freshness is the point.
2. **Positive** for both general and business users.
3. **Easy to apply** — teachable in ~3 steps; the viewer can do it today.
4. **Concrete payoff** — they end with something (a tool, a doc, an asset), not just "an answer".
Verify the facts from 1–2 credible sources before building (match the tool's own wording for stats).

## The on-screen arc (mirrors the video scenes)
Hook → the Number/news → What it does → The math (old vs new) → How to (3 steps) → The rule → CTA + loop.
Keep on-graphic lines ≤ ~19 characters where they're big headlines, so they fit. One gold idea per scene.

## Caption formula (longer than on-graphic; still AC's voice)
1. **Hook** — contrarian + the news + one big number.
2. **Reframe** — flip the obvious reading.
3. **Plain-English breakdown** — old way / new way, then the 3-step how-to with → arrows.
4. **The rule** — a repeatable principle ("Stop making PDFs. Start making tools.").
5. **CTA** — em-dash, soft: Save → Follow, plus a question to pull comments. No hard sell.
Formatting tics: line breaks every 1–2 sentences, one big number, a question near the end.

## Hashtags
Default to the **5 "algorithm" tags** Austin asks for: one broad-topical (rides the tool, e.g. `#Canva`),
one capability (`#NoCode`), one category (`#AItools`), two audience (`#SmallBusiness`, `#AIforBusiness`).
Tight and relevant beats 30 generic tags. Offer the fuller ~12-tag `ac-instagram` bank if he wants it.

## Non-negotiables (brand guardrails)
Black canvas, one gold accent per scene, Lora headlines + Poppins labels only. Plain English —
kill every buzzword and every "this changes everything". No hard selling in feed. Keep @itsac.ai on every frame.
