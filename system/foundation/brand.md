- **Lora** — headlines and big numbers. High-contrast serif; this is the "luxury" in black and gold.

## Voice

- Direct and plain-spoken. Short sentences. If a 12-year-old couldn't follow it, rewrite it.
- Contrarian but constructive. Challenge the obvious take, then give a better one. Never doom.
- Anchored to one real, current number. The number earns attention; plain English keeps it.
- Encouraging, not preachy. Anti-hype, pro-action.

## Non-negotiables

- One gold accent per scene. Gold means "look here."
- No em-dashes on screen, ever. Period, comma, or line break.
- No buzzwords, no "this changes everything," no fear-mongering.
- ~30 words max per frame; keep the black breathing room.
- No other colors, stock photos, or clip-art. Name a company with a clean gold wordmark, never a
  multicolor logo.
- `@itsac.ai` on every frame. Never two handles on one asset.
