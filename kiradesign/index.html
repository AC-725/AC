How we work

Most studios hand your drawings to someone else.

We own all three companies, so the people building it answer to the people who drew it. One programme, one point of accountability, and no gap for a variation order to live in.

01 — Design KIRA Design Ltd

Concept, spatial planning and detailed design. Every project starts with dialogue — how a family actually moves through a home, before a single line is drawn.

02 — Build Times Creative Construction Ltd

Our own contracting arm. Site works, joinery and finishes run to the same standard the drawings were held to, on a programme we control.

03 — Furnish MOï Home Collection Ltd

Furniture, lighting and art curated in-house — so the finished room matches the one you were shown, down to the fitting.

One conversation, carried all the way through.

Material & craft

Interior design is invisible until you point at it.

Every project begins on a material board like this one. Naming the decisions is how a client comes to see what they're paying for.

Fluted oak Vertical battens that lengthen a low ceiling and soften every echo in the room.
Book-matched walnut Grain mirrored across the joint, so the wall reads as one board instead of four.
Honed limestone Matte, so afternoon glare off the water never blows out the floor.
Champagne metal Warmer than brass, quieter than gold. It holds a warm-lit room together.
Speckled granite A dark anchor underfoot. Every warm palette needs one cold, heavy thing.
Lacquer red The wall you meet first in our studio. Auspicious, and impossible to forget.
