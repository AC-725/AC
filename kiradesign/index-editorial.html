How we work

Most studios hand your drawings to someone else.

We own all three companies, so the people building it answer to the people who drew it. One programme, one point of accountability, and no gap for a variation order to live in.

One conversation, carried all the way through.

A separate contractor introduces a second set of interests, a second programme and a second version of the truth. Owning the build removes all three at once — which is why developers come back to us for the next tower rather than re-tendering.

Material & craft

Interior design is invisible until you point at it.

Every project begins on a material board. Naming each decision is how a client comes to see what they are paying for.

Fluted oakVertical battens lengthen a low ceiling and soften every echo in the room.
Book-matched walnutGrain mirrored across the joint, so the wall reads as one board instead of four.
Honed limestoneMatte, so afternoon glare off the water never blows out the floor.
Champagne metalWarmer than brass, quieter than gold. It holds a warm-lit room together.
Speckled graniteA dark anchor underfoot. Every warm palette needs one cold, heavy thing.
Lacquer redThe wall you meet first in our studio. Auspicious, and impossible to forget.
