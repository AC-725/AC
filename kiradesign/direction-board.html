Taken from the rooms, not from a theme.

Your signature projects are all water-facing — Grand Marine, Mayfair By The Sea, Victoria Voyage, Marinella, the Tsing Yi duplex. In person they all read the same way: warm walnut, stone and brass wrapped around full-height glass full of cold grey-green harbour. So the neutrals run warm and the single accent runs cold. That tension is the brand.

04  Motion doctrine

You said the animations were bad. Here's the rule set that prevents it.

Bad motion on luxury sites is almost never too little — it's too many kinds at once. This is a doctrine a developer can be held to.

01

One curve, everywhere

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) — the soft-close drawer. No linear, no ease-in-out, no exceptions.

02

One property, one direction

Reveals are opacity plus a 14px rise. Never scale, blur, rotate and slide at once — that combination is the thing that reads as cheap.

03

Nothing loops

No infinite animation anywhere on the site. No bouncing scroll cues, no drifting gradients, no ken-burns on stills.

04

Room pace, not UI pace

600–900ms. The speed of walking into a space and letting your eye settle — not a 200ms button state.

05

Motion reveals, never decorates

If an animation isn't disclosing content or confirming an action, it gets cut. That single test removes most of what's wrong now.

06

Mobile gets less

Scroll-linked effects off below 768px, prefers-reduced-motion fully honoured. Directly addresses the mobile complaint.

Section 5 — Material & detail This is the section that makes the "interior design advantage" visible. Nothing on the current site does this job.

The part clients feel but can't name

Interior design is invisible until you point at it.

Book-matched walnut Grain mirrored across the joint so the wall reads as one board.
Honed limestone Matte, so afternoon glare off the water doesn't blow out the floor.
Brushed brass Unlacquered, left to patinate. It gets better, not worse.
Sea glass Low-iron glazing — the harbour stays grey-green instead of turning bottle-green.
Concealed cove Light source hidden; you see the wall lit, never the fitting.
