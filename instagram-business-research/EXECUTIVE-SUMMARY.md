## The one-paragraph version

Your positioning is right and the market data proves it — **57% of Hong Kong SMEs
who haven't adopted AI say the blocker is "lack of relevant knowledge or skills."**
That is literally your product. But the research surfaced four things working
against you, and none of them are about content quality: (1) you're optimising for
likes when the 2026 algorithm pays for **sends**, (2) your hashtag habit is actively
costing reach, (3) your greatest brand strength — the identical black-and-gold
template every day — is now a **medium platform risk**, and (4) every competitor
studied makes their money off an **owned list**, not off Instagram. Fix those four
and the page stops being a portfolio and starts being a pipeline.

---

## 1. The algorithm changed what it pays for. Your posts optimise for the old thing.

Mosseri's three confirmed ranking signals for 2026 are **watch time, likes per
reach, and sends per reach** — and a DM send is worth roughly **3–5x a like** for
reaching new people. Sends drive *non-follower* reach specifically; likes mostly
help you with people who already follow you.

Your current format — one big stat, elegantly set — is built to be *admired*. It
needs to be built to be **forwarded to a colleague**.

**Amend:** end each stat post with a line engineered for the send, not the like.
Not "Save this" (saves help, but sends outrank them) — something closer to
*"Send this to the person who keeps saying AI is a fad."* Your existing CTA ladder
in Part 3 should be reordered: **Send → Save → Follow → Comment → DM.**

**Targets:** sends per reach ≥1% (good) / ≥2% (excellent); saves per reach ≥1.5%
on carousels; ≥50% of Reel reach from non-followers.

---

## 2. Hashtags are costing you reach. Keywords are the replacement.

This directly contradicts the hashtag banks in your Part 2 protocol and the
"5 trending hashtags" your reel skill ships with every video:

- Posts relying on hashtags saw **32% fewer views and 34% fewer interactions**
  (2026 study).
- **Mosseri has stated outright** that hashtags "won't necessarily increase your
  reach." Instagram removed hashtag *following* entirely.
- In-app search now parses **captions, spoken audio, on-screen text, alt text, and
  your bio** — not hashtags.
- Since **2025-07-10**, public professional-account content is **indexed by Google
  and Bing** (on by default; covers posts back to 2020).

**Amend:** cut to 3–5 hashtags maximum, treat them as topic labels not a reach
strategy, and move the effort to a **keyword map**. Put a real search phrase in
**caption line 1**, say it **out loud in the Reel** (audio is parsed), put it in
**on-screen text**, and fill **alt text** on every post.

Target phrases nobody in your niche is contesting: *"AI for small business Hong
Kong," "AI consultant Hong Kong," "AI training for SMEs," "ChatGPT for small
business,"* plus 繁中 equivalents. A sub-10k account can genuinely win these —
they're long-tail and geo-locked, which is exactly where a small account beats a
big generic one.

> **This is the highest-leverage, lowest-effort change in this entire document.**
> It costs you nothing and it makes every post you've already made discoverable.

## 3. Your visual consistency is now a platform risk. (The uncomfortable one.)

Your protocol's non-negotiable is *"the repetition **is** the branding."* Two
policy changes since December have put that in tension with reach:

- **2025-12-10:** algorithm reset. Aggregator/repost accounts reportedly lost
  60–80% reach; original creators gained 40–60%. *(Third-party figures, not Meta.)*
- **2026-04-30:** Mosseri expanded the originality policy — accounts primarily
  sharing content they didn't create are removed from **all** recommendation
  surfaces, across **every** format (previously Reels-only).
- **May 2026:** opt-in "AI Creator" profile labels join the automatic "AI info"
  labels.

**Your actual risk profile:**

| Risk | Level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregator penalty | **LOW** | Your Reels are self-made and original |
| Templated-content deprioritisation | **MEDIUM** | Identical daily template is the exact pattern being demoted |
| AI-label reach penalty | **LOW** | Meta states labels carry no algorithmic penalty |
| Deceptive photorealistic AI | **HIGH** — but only if you post it | You never should. Keep it that way |

**Amend — and this does not break your brand:** keep the palette, the fonts, the
gold accent, the monogram. Rotate **at least 3 structural template variants**
(stat-led / chart-led / quote-led) instead of one, and post **at least 4 pieces a
month with your real face or voice**. Meta is penalising *sameness of production*,
not *consistency of identity*. A recognisable brand that varies its layouts is
safe; a page that looks auto-generated is not.

The face requirement has a second payoff: 38% of HK SMEs say the barrier is
**"lack of reliable external support."** They're buying a person they trust. A
black canvas can't do that job on its own.

## 4. Format mix and cadence — the numbers you're being scored against

| Metric | 2026 benchmark | Your target (sub-10k) |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement rate (per followers) | ~0.45–0.50% platform average | **≥4%** (nano median), stretch 6% |
| Reels reach rate | ~30.8% of followers, **2x** every other format | ≥25% |
| Non-follower share of Reel views | 60%+ platform norm | ≥50% |
| Carousel engagement per reach | ~6.90% (best of any format) | ≥6% |
| Single images | **–17% YoY** — declining | Avoid as a primary format |
| Cadence | 3–5 feed posts/week is the growth threshold | 2–4 Reels + 1–2 carousels/week |

**Reels are your discovery engine; carousels are your conversion engine.** Reels
get in front of strangers (60%+ non-follower views), carousels earn the saves and
depth that turn a stranger into a follower. 30–60 second Reels get the highest
reach of any Reel length.

**Free tooling you're not using:**

- **Trial Reels** — show a Reel to non-followers *only* for ~72 hours, zero risk to
  your grid. Run **8+/month**, graduate the winners.
  *Correction worth knowing:* the widely-quoted "80% increase in non-follower
  reach" is a **misreading** of Instagram's data. The accurate claim: 40% of
  creators who tried Trial Reels posted more often, and 80% *of those* saw
  increased non-follower reach. Still worth running — just don't expect the
  headline number.
- **Edits** (Meta's free app, 130+ features shipped in year one) — teleprompter,
  voice enhancer, per-Reel **skip rate and retention curves**, and auto bilingual
  captions in 15 languages. Replaces CapCut Pro at zero cost. The retention curve
  is how you learn which hooks actually hold — use it to rewrite at least one hook
  a month.

## 5. Bilingual EN + 繁體中文 is your moat. Nobody else can copy it.

This is the finding I'd act on first after the keyword fix.

- HK campaigns running **both** English and Traditional Chinese report engagement
  **20–30% higher** than single-language.
- **4,659,200 Instagram users in Hong Kong** — 60.7% of the population; largest
  cohort is 25–34 (1.67M), i.e. working decision-makers.
- ~88% of HK speaks Cantonese; English is the business language. Traditional
  Chinese resonates specifically in business/financial contexts.

God of Prompt, Superhuman, The Rundown — none of them can serve this audience.
It's the one advantage that isn't a matter of out-working someone.

**Amend:** bilingual captions on 100% of feed posts, and **≥4 TC-led posts/month**
written natively in Chinese — *parallel content, not translation*. A translated
English post reads like a translated English post.

## 6. Instagram is not where the money is. Every competitor proves it.

The clearest pattern across all six accounts studied:

| Brand | Instagram | Where the money actually is |
|---|---|---|
| **Ole Lehmann** | only **~11K** | **~$450K** in course launches off a newsletter |
| **Superhuman AI** | ~73K (weak) | 1.5M+ email list, 7-figure revenue, **63% decision-makers** |
| **The Rundown AI** | 551K | ~$10M/yr run-rate; ~2M list at 50–52% open rates |
| **God of Prompt** | ~342K | $150 lifetime bundle via **comment-keyword DM funnels** |
| **Liam Ottley** | ~40K | Free 329K Skool community → $99/mo → $5–7K coaching |

Lehmann is the one to internalise: **11K Instagram followers, ~$450K in revenue.**
Follower count is not the business. The owned list is.

**The funnel to build:**

```
Reel (discovery, non-followers)
  → carousel (depth, saves)
    → comment keyword
      → DM automation (~90% open, up to 28% CTR vs 2–3% for email)
        → WhatsApp for high-intent   ← HK's default business channel
          → discovery call → paid audit → retainer
```

Two operational notes: **ManyChat's free plan was cut from 1,000 to 25 contacts on
2026-03-02** (Essential is $14/mo for 250 — start there, not free). And Meta
enforces a **24-hour messaging window** plus ~200 automated DMs/hour.

**Lead magnet:** interactive beats PDF by **~32%**. For an AI consultant the lead
magnet *is* the product demo — build a custom GPT ("Find the 3 tasks in your
business AI should be doing") that asks 2–3 qualifying questions and returns a
personalised answer. It proves your competence in the act of being used.

**Target:** 50–150 new owned-list contacts/month.

## 8. LinkedIn is where B2B actually closes

Instagram builds the audience; LinkedIn closes the deal. **83% of a B2B purchase
process happens before the buyer ever contacts you**, and 95% of purchases go to a
day-one shortlist vendor — so you need to be visible *during* the research phase.

Personal profiles get up to **8x the engagement and 561% more reach** than company
pages posting identical content; median engagement is **4.7% on personal profiles**
vs 1–2% on company pages. This is the cheapest possible win: **repurpose 100% of
your Instagram educational content to your personal LinkedIn within 24 hours.**
Same words, no new production. Superhuman grew substantially on repurposed
LinkedIn carousels.

**Target:** 8–12 LinkedIn posts/month; 1–2 discovery calls/month sourced from
LinkedIn by month 4–6.

## 9. Profile & website amendments

> **Note on the website:** I could not load `ac-wins.com` — this environment's

> `www.ac-wins.com`). The recommendations below are grounded in your brand

**Name field** — this is heavily weighted in Instagram search, and it's currently
spending its budget on your name:

```
Austin · AI in Plain English          ← current
AI for Small Business · Hong Kong     ← searchable
```

Your handle already says "ac.wins" and your avatar is the monogram — the name field
is better spent on the phrase a stranger types.

**Bio** — the current four-beat shape is good; only the CTA is weak. *"👇 Start
with AC"* tells a stranger nothing about what happens if they tap. Make the last
line name the actual thing they get:

```
Making AI make sense for normal businesses.
📈 Daily AI market trends · zero jargon
🇭🇰 Hong Kong → worldwide
👇 Free: find 3 tasks AI should be doing for you
```

**Link in bio** — category-labeled, visually organised link pages convert **~34%
better** than bare link lists. Order by funnel stage, lead with the free thing:
*Free AI audit → Work with me → Daily trends → LinkedIn → WhatsApp.*

**Highlights** — your existing six are well-designed. One reprioritisation: move
**"Wins"** (proof) to position 2, directly after "Start Here." Since 38% of HK SMEs
cite *lack of reliable external support* as their blocker, proof is the buying
trigger — it shouldn't sit in fourth place. Add TC-language versions of the
covers as the bilingual push rolls out.

**For the website, the research says a consulting site needs:** a specific outcome
in the headline (not "AI consulting" — something like *"AI that saves your team 10
hours a week"*), named proof with real numbers, the workshop offer with a price or
a range visible, a WhatsApp contact button (HK's default business channel — a
contact form is friction here), bilingual EN/繁中, and the same interactive lead
magnet as the bio link. Confirm Google indexing is **on** at Settings → Privacy →
"Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results" — it's on by
default, but worth verifying, since it's what makes your Reels findable on Google.
