# AC Wins — WhatsApp & Email Reply Drafts

Turn a client's incoming thread into ready-to-send reply drafts in the right language and the right voice.

**First: read `references/quality-rules.md` and obey it throughout. Rule 4 is absolute here: draft, never send.**

## Process

```
Receive thread → Identify client & voice → Understand → Draft → Deliver
```

### 1. Receive

Accept a pasted conversation, screenshot, or email chain. Identify who's who (the business vs. the customer) — ask if unclear.

### 2. Voice & language

- **Language:** draft in the language of the incoming thread by default. EN / 繁體中文 / 简体中文 variants on request. Mixed-language threads get a reply matching the customer's dominant language.
- **Voice:** the *client's business* voice as recorded in their `client-notes.md` (formality, greetings, sign-offs, emoji habits). No notes → AC Wins default: plain, warm, short sentences, no jargon, no hype.
- 中文 drafts use full-width punctuation（，。：）and natural phrasing — never literal translation of an English draft.

### 3. Understand before drafting

Extract what the customer actually needs: the question, the complaint, the deadline, the amount. Facts pulled from the thread that are uncertain (a date that could be read two ways, an unclear amount) → `[?]` in the draft and listed in the verify-checklist. **Never invent prices, stock levels, dates, or commitments** — mark gaps as `[fill in: delivery date]` for the user.

### 4. Draft

- Routine message → one solid draft.
- Tricky message (complaint, refund request, angry customer, negotiation) → **2–3 options with different tones** (e.g. apologize-and-fix, hold-firm-politely, ask-for-details), each labeled with when to use it.
- Keep WhatsApp drafts WhatsApp-length. Emails get subject lines.

### 5. Deliver

`reply-drafts.md` at the standard output location: the drafts, any `[fill in: …]` gaps, and the "Verify before sending" checklist. Append voice/tone learnings to `client-notes.md`.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sending, scheduling, or "test-sending" anything | Never. Drafts only — the user sends. |
| Inventing a delivery date or price to sound helpful | `[fill in: …]` placeholder; the user knows the answer. |
| Translating an English draft into 中文 word-for-word | Write the 中文 natively from the same intent. |
| One tone for an angry customer | 2–3 labeled options; tone is a business decision. |
| Replying to the wrong side of the thread | Confirm who the client is before drafting. |
