# VO hook banks — spoken openings, synced to scene A

**Added 2026-08-18 at AC's request**, adapted from a high-engagement creator's hook
library (creatorzone.io carousels, screenshotted by AC). These are FORMULAS for the
**spoken VO opening line**, written first, with the on-screen scene-A headline mirroring
the same beat. They compose with `hooks.md` — every law there still applies (payoff
first, real non-round number, stake left open, no em-dash on screen, blacklist).
This file adds *phrasings*; `hooks.md` stays the grader.

## Workflow (AC's chosen mode: offer 3 per run)

At the creative checkpoint of every video run (TOD and News), present **3 written VO
hooks from 3 different banks**, each already:
1. filled with the run's verified numbers,
2. written to scene A's syllable budget (News A: 9–11 syl / 2.6s · TOD A: 12–14 syl / 3.6s),
3. paired with its mirrored on-screen headline (≤7 words, ≤~16 chars/line),
4. graded stop/send per `hooks.md`.

AC picks one; the two runners-up become the A/B trial-reel variants on the beat sheet.
Rotate the offered banks so consecutive days never open from the same bank —
log the used bank in `run-log.md` next to the hook grade.

**VO-sync rule:** the hook is a SPOKEN line first. Check it with `vo_budget.py` before
the checkpoint; the on-screen headline carries the same claim in fewer words, landing
fully legible by 2.3s. Never write a screen headline first and bolt VO onto it.

## The four banks (AC-calibrated)

Each formula below is rewritten for the page's law: anti-hype, one verified number,
plain English. **First-person claims ("I tested…", "I read…") may only ship when they
are literally true from a logged run** — the Day 28 free-tier check ("we checked 14
tools") is the model. If the claim is a study's, name the study, never "I".

### 1 · Storytelling (best for: TOD, method posts)

| Formula (VO) | AC calibration |
|---|---|
| "I [did action] every day for [X] days. This is what happened." | Only for real logged experiments. Strongest TOD variant. |
| "I [checked/tested] [X] [things]. Don't waste your time. Here's what actually worked." | The house receipts move — Day 28's exact shape. |
| "I've [read/watched] over [X] [things] on [topic]. Most were useless. These changed how I think." | The count must be real. |
| "If I woke up tomorrow [in state] and wanted [result] in [time], here's exactly what I'd do." | Good for evergreen "start here" TODs. |
| "Here's how you turn [small number] into [bigger number]." | Both numbers verified, non-round. |
| "I love [tool], but [boring action] sucks. Here are [X] ways to make it suck less." | Honest-limitation opener; very on-brand. |
| "It took [X] years to build. I'll teach you the [N] lessons in [X] seconds." | Attribute the years honestly (the tool's, not AC's, unless true). |
| ~~"I really hope I'm not the first person to tell you this but…"~~ | **Skip** — tease with no payoff; violates payoff-first. |

### 2 · Controversy (best for: News, myth-kills — the page's home turf)

| Formula (VO) | AC calibration |
|---|---|
| "One of the biggest scams in [niche] is [popular thing]." | The anti-hype flagship. Must be provable — receipts on screen by scene C. |
| "People make [topic] sound complicated. Here's the simple version." | Pure brand promise. |
| "If you can't [result], it's not because you're not [trait]. You don't know how to [action]." | The direct-callout (hooks.md style 3) in bank form. |
| "There is no reason to be [doing common action] every day." | Needs the replacement shown by scene B. |
| "Your first [result] won't come from [popular method]." | Dated/verified counter-example required. |
| "You don't need [trait/budget] to [result]." | Free-tier stories. |
| "The easiest way to [negative outcome] is [action]." | Warning form; keep the fix in the same reel. |
| ~~"I don't know who needs to hear this, but your [thing] sucks."~~ | **Skip** — burned phrasing + punches the viewer, not the hype. |

### 3 · Curiosity (best for: News with a fresh study, TOD discovery)

| Formula (VO) | AC calibration |
|---|---|
| "A new study found [stat]. Here's what they didn't tell you." | The page's bread and butter — stat verified to primary source, the "didn't tell you" part must actually be in the source. |
| "Everyone tells you to [popular advice]. Nobody shows you how." | Then SHOW it — TOD steps carry the how. |
| "Here's a decade of [niche] lessons in [X] seconds." | Attribute whose decade. |
| "This one [tool/prompt] does more than [expensive thing]." | Price-anchored; both sides verified (the $20-vs-free move, Day 24's shape). |
| "Give me [X] seconds and I'll show you [skill]." | State the real runtime; the reel must deliver inside it. |
| "I'll take you from zero to [result] with [N] steps." | N = the TOD's actual step count (3). |
| ~~"Here's the one thing successful people do differently."~~ | **Skip** — vague superlative, no number, any-page hook. |

### 4 · Authority (best for: rules, habits, the page's "laws")

| Formula (VO) | AC calibration |
|---|---|
| "There's one habit silently destroying your [result]." | Name it by scene B; evidence by C. |
| "Here's a [X]-minute habit that measurably improves [outcome]." | "Measurably" means a number on screen. |
| "One thing makes your [result] [X] times better." | The X must come from a source, not vibes. |
| "Here's how I make [affordable thing] work like [expensive thing]." | Free-tier positioning; receipts. |
| "In the next [X] seconds, you'll see [hidden information]." | Deliver inside the runtime. |
| "Remember [simple concept] before your next [action]." | The "rule" scene D as an opener. |
| ~~"99% of your success depends on this one thing."~~ | **Skip** — blacklisted shape ("99% of people…"), unverifiable. |
| ~~"What if I told you [result]?"~~ | **Skip** — burned meta-tease. |

## Grading and the scorecard

Bank formulas are raw material, not free passes: every filled hook still gets the
`hooks.md` v2 grade (stop A–F / send A–F + motive) and feeds the same virality
scorecard row. A bank hook that fills with a vague or unverifiable number grades C
and does not ship, whatever the formula's pedigree. The meta-rule stands: **if the
filled hook could appear on any AI page, it is dead** — the fill (your number, your
receipt, your tool) is what makes it AC's.

## Worked example — a TOD (structure only; numbers come from the day's research)

Story: [tool] does [job] free; the paid tier everyone buys costs [$X/mo].

- **Storytelling:** "I tested [tool] on [real task] for [N] days. Here's what broke."
  → screen: `[N] days. / One thing broke.` (12 syl ✓ TOD A)
- **Controversy:** "One of the biggest scams in AI tools is paying for [job]."
  → screen: `Stop paying / for [job].`
- **Curiosity:** "Everyone says use [tool]. Nobody shows the [setting] that matters."
  → screen: `The [setting] / nobody shows.`

Present all 3 at checkpoint · AC picks · runners-up = A/B variants · log the bank.
