# Foundation — profile, visual system, Highlights

The fixed brand. This Part covers how the page is set up and how it looks: the
profile and bio, the exact visual system every graphic follows, and the six
Story Highlights. Nothing here changes often — it's the frame the daily content
lives inside.

**Read this Part for:** bio and link-in-bio edits, checking a graphic or idea
against the brand, post layout questions, adding or reordering Highlights, or
the first-time launch checklist.

---

## Launch checklist — the 3-step setup

The whole launch fits in three moves:

1. **Set up the profile.** Create the account, drop in the avatar, name, bio,
   and handle from the Profile Setup below. Add the six highlight covers.
2. **Load the 7 graphics.** Save the seven post images (delivered as PNGs).
   They're numbered Day 1–7 in posting order.
3. **Post daily.** Follow the 7-day calendar in `content.md`. Copy each caption +
   hashtags with one tap. Show up every day for seven days.

## Visual Identity

One system, infinitely repeatable. Every post = **black canvas, one gold idea,
elegant serif headline.** That consistency is what turns a grid into a brand.
When checking any graphic, it should pass all three sections below.

### Palette

Use these exact values. Gold is the only accent — it always means "look here."

| Hex | Name | Where it's used |
|-----|------|-----------------|
| `#0A0A0A` | Base Black | Background of every post |
| `#141210` | Warm Charcoal | Subtle depth / gradient |
| `#E7C765` | Signal Gold | Primary accent & monogram |
| `#C9A23F` | Deep Gold | Gradient mid-tone |
| `#F4DF95` | Light Gold | Gradient highlight / sheen |
| `#F4F0E7` | Warm White | Main headline text |
| `#B9B1A1` | Muted Stone | Body / supporting text |

### Typography

Two fonts, never more — the repetition is the branding.

- **Lora** — headlines & big numbers. Elegant high-contrast serif; this is the
  "luxury" in black & gold. Weights 400–700.
  *e.g.* `$347B · The winners just started.`
- **Poppins** — labels, kickers & body. Clean geometric sans for maximum
  clarity. Weights 400–700.
  *e.g.* `DAILY AI TRENDS · NO HYPE, NO JARGON`

### Post anatomy — the repeatable template

Every graphic is built from the same parts:

- Thin gold frame + corner ticks on a near-black canvas.
- Top kicker: content pillar (left) + day counter (right).
- One big idea: a serif statement **or** a gold stat number.
- One short supporting line in muted stone.
- Footer: AC monogram + `@itsac.ai` (the signature).

### Keep it disciplined

**Do**

- One gold accent per post — gold means "look here."
- Lots of black breathing room; ~30 words max on-image.

**Don't**

- Add extra colors, stock photos, or clip-art.
- Crowd the frame or shrink the headline to fit more in.
- Change the fonts — the repetition *is* the branding.

## Story Highlights

Six covers, already designed and delivered as PNGs. Instagram crops them to a
circle, so the gold icons are centered to survive the crop. Add them in this
order — the order tells a story from "who is this" to "how do I work with you."

1. **Start Here** — pin the intro: who Austin is, what the page gives (daily AI
   trends, no jargon), and the best 2–3 posts.
2. **AI Trends** — save every daily trend post here so new visitors can binge
   the value in one tap.
3. **Myth-Busts** — the reality-checks: hype vs. truth. This is where the "no
   BS" reputation gets built.
4. **Wins** — client results, before/after, screenshots of time saved. Proof
   beats promises.
5. **AI 101** — beginner basics and tool explainers for people just starting
   out.
6. **Work w/ Me** — how Austin helps, who it's for, and how to reach him. Soft,
   not salesy.

> Highlights are also where the soft offer lives. Keep "Work w/ Me" genuinely
> helpful in tone — it's the bottom of the CTA ladder (see `growth.md`), not a pitch.
