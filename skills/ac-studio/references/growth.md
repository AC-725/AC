# Growth — cadence, engagement, CTA ladder

The growth playbook. Content is only half the job — this Part is how a
brand-new page actually gains traction in the first 30 days and beyond. It's
about the reps around the posts: cadence, engagement, rotation, and the soft
path from stranger to conversation.

**Read this Part for:** growth and reach questions, weekly planning, building a
posting habit, or turning followers into DMs without hard-selling.

---

## Cadence & consistency

Reliability beats intensity. The algorithm and the audience both reward a page
they can count on.

- **Same time, every day.** Predictability is a feature — it trains both the
  algorithm and the audience to expect you.
- **Treat one week as a "season."** Batch-make on Sundays so a busy day never
  breaks the streak.
- **Aim for 5 singles + 2 Reels per week** once the rhythm is there. Reels do
  the reach; singles do the depth.

---

## Engagement — the real hack

Posting is only the trigger; engagement is what actually spreads a post. This is
where most new pages underinvest.

- **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes.** Early engagement signals
  the post is worth showing to more people — so be present right after posting.
- **Warm up before you post.** Spend ~10 minutes commenting thoughtfully on
  bigger AI pages first; it puts the page in front of the right audience.
- **End captions with a question.** Comments beat likes for reach, and a genuine
  question is the simplest way to earn them.

---

## Rotate these pillars

Variety keeps the page fresh; rotation keeps it recognizable. Cycle through the
content pillars rather than posting the same angle twice in a row:

- The Big Picture · The Shift · Real Talk (myth-bust)
- For Small Business · Tool of the Day · Austin's Take

And keep the value compounding: **save every trend post into the "AI Trends"
Highlight** so a new visitor can binge a week of value in one tap.

---

## The CTA ladder — no hard selling

The offer never gets pushed in the feed. Instead, let the value pull people down
the ladder on its own — each rung is a slightly bigger ask, and people take them
in their own time:

1. **Save** — the lowest-friction yes; it also boosts reach.
2. **Follow** — they want more of the daily value.
3. **Comment** — now they're in the conversation.
4. **DM** — the door to working together.

Only mention working together in **Highlights and DMs** — never in the daily
posts. The feed earns trust; the offer lives one layer deeper, so it never feels
like a pitch. This is the whole reason the page can sell without ever sounding
salesy.

---

## The first 30 days, in practice

Put the pieces together and the first month looks like this: post daily at the
same times, run at least a couple of Reels a week, spend the 10 minutes before
and 60 minutes after each post actually engaging, rotate the pillars so the grid
stays varied, and funnel every trend post into the AI Trends Highlight. Growth
on a new page is slow-then-sudden — the compounding comes from showing up long
enough for the reps to add up. The single most common failure is quitting before
that compounding kicks in, which is exactly why the Day 7 post promises to be
there daily "to help you not quit." Consistency is the strategy.
