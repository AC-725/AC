# Number check — Goldman Sachs 76% / 14%

**Status: VERIFIED at primary source.** Safe for screen.

## Primary
Goldman Sachs press release, 17 Mar 2026 — "Survey: Small Businesses Embrace AI — But Need Training and Support to Fully Harness It"
https://www.goldmansachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2026/small-businesses-embrace-ai-but-need-training-and-support-to-fully-harness-it

Secondary (same publisher): 10,000 Small Businesses Voices insight page, 17 Mar 2026
https://www.goldmansachs.com/community-transformation/10000-small-businesses-voices/insights/ai-presents-a-major-opportunity-for-small-businesses

## Wording to match (verbatim from source)
- "More than three-quarters of small businesses (76%) report currently using AI"
- "Only 14% of small businesses are fully integrating AI into core operations"
- "Ninety-three percent of those using AI say it has had a positive impact on their business"
- "84% cite increased efficiency and productivity as the primary benefit"
- "73% of small businesses would benefit from additional training and implementation resources"

## Method
- n = 1,256 Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses participants
- Fielded by Babson College and David Binder Research, 27 Jan – 4 Feb 2026
- All 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico

## Caveats for the copy
- Sample is 10KSB alumni, not a random US sample. Say "small businesses surveyed" or cite Goldman by name. Never "of all small businesses".
- "Fully integrating into core operations" is the source's phrase. On screen: "put it to work" / "in core operations" is fair; "use it properly" is not.
- 93% and 84% are of AI USERS, not of all respondents.
- Source date for the footnote: **Mar 2026** (publish), fieldwork Jan–Feb 2026. Not Sep.

## Footnote line (every slide, reel)
Source: Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses Voices, Mar 2026, n=1,256
