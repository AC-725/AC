# Conflict & attention checks

The attention list is the reason the client keeps using this. Run every check that the available data supports; skip a check silently only when the data for it is absent (e.g. no roster → no staffing checks), and say so in the brief.

Each finding is **one line: the fact, then the suggested action.** Calm, specific, actionable. Not "⚠️ CONFLICT!!!" — more like "Two site visits at 10:00 Tue (Kwai Chung and Fo Tan) — one needs moving."

## Order (most urgent first)

1. **Overdue** — a deadline before today, not marked done. → chase / confirm today.
2. **Due today** — deadline or commitment dated today. → confirm it's handled.
3. **Clashes** — see below.
4. **Staffing** — understaffed, single-cover, coverage gap.
5. **Unconfirmed** — a "TBC"/"to confirm" time on something happening soon.

## The checks

**Double-booking / overlap** — same person or resource in two items whose times overlap. Report both, with times and locations.

**Back-to-back, no travel buffer** — two appointments at *different* locations less than a sensible buffer apart (default 45 min in Hong Kong; adjust if the client says otherwise). → flag the tighter of the two.

**Understaffed shift** — fewer staff rostered than the client's stated minimum for that day/shift. If no minimum was given, don't invent one — instead flag days that are visibly thinner than the week's norm and say the minimum is assumed.

**Single-cover / key-person risk** — only one person can open, close, or run a needed function that day, or one named person appears on every critical item. → note the risk; suggest a backup.

**Coverage gap** — a day inside the window, within opening hours, with nobody rostered. → flag clearly; this is the one owners most want caught.

**Deadline with no owner** — a `deadline` item with `who` blank. → ask who owns it; unowned deadlines slip.

**Deadline landing on a thin day** — a real due date on a day that's also understaffed or has a coverage gap. → surface the collision; it's worse than either alone.

**Unconfirmed / tentative** — `start = TBC` or a note like "to confirm" on something within ~48 hours. → nudge to lock the time.

## Phrasing

- Say what, when, and where in the fewest words that are still unambiguous.
- Always pair the fact with a next step the owner can take.
- If a finding rests on an assumption (an assumed staffing minimum, an assumed travel time), say so in the same line — a flagged assumption is honest; a hidden one is a trap.
- If nothing needs attention, say that plainly ("No clashes or gaps this week — everything's covered."). A clean week is a real, reassuring result, not an empty section.
