# Scoring Rubric

Owner-editable. Adjust anchors and weights here as judgment evolves — the skill reads this file, not hardcoded numbers.

## Step 1 — The 2-week gate (hard filter)

**Gate test:** Can we demonstrate measurable results within 2 weeks using tools the client already has (WhatsApp, Excel, email, existing SaaS)? **Yes / No.**

- **No** → candidate goes to the Phase 2+ list in the internal brief. It is not scored. It is not silently dropped.
- The gate is a filter, not a scoring factor. Feasibility never trades off against impact.

Common gate failures: needs new hardware, needs a system the client doesn't have, needs staff retraining longer than the pilot, needs another party (supplier, bank, government) to change behavior, results not measurable inside 2 weeks.

## Step 2 — Score survivors (1–5 on three factors, equal weights)

### Hours saved (per week, across all affected staff)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | < 2 hrs/week |
| 2 | 2–5 hrs/week |
| 3 | 5–10 hrs/week |
| 4 | 10–15 hrs/week |
| 5 | > 15 hrs/week |

### Error/risk cut

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cosmetic — errors rare or harmless |
| 2 | Occasional rework, minor cost |
| 3 | Regular errors costing real time or customer goodwill |
| 4 | Errors hit money directly (wrong invoices, missed orders) |
| 5 | Errors risk losing clients, compliance trouble, or serious money |

### Change-readiness

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Staff resistant, owner skeptical, no champion |
| 2 | Owner willing but staff wary |
| 3 | Neutral — will follow if it works |
| 4 | Staff frustrated with status quo, open to change |
| 5 | Actively asking for this; internal champion exists |

## Step 3 — Decide

- **Total = sum of three scores** (equal weights).
- **Tie-breaker:** higher Hours saved wins.
- Name exactly **one winner** (the recommendation) and **one runner-up** (named in both outputs, recommended in neither).
